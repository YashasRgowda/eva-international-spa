Good to Know

Frequently Asked
Questions

Where is EVA International Spa located?

EVA International Spa is located in Koramangala 7th Block, Bengaluru.

What massage services do you offer?

We offer Deep Tissue, Swedish, Thai, Aromatherapy and Balinese massages, along with Body Scrub, Couples Spa and Jacuzzi experiences.

Do you offer full-body massages?

Yes, we offer several full-body massage options, including Swedish, Balinese, Thai and Deep Tissue treatments.

Do you have couples spa services?

Yes, our Couples Spa experience includes two massages side by side in a private room.

How long does a massage session take?

Our massage sessions range from 60 to 90 minutes, depending on the selected treatment.

Is Jacuzzi available?

Yes, Jacuzzi is available as a separate relaxation experience or as part of a selected spa package.

Do I need to book an appointment?

We recommend booking in advance to confirm your preferred treatment and appointment time.

How can I book a spa appointment?

You can call or WhatsApp our team to enquire about treatments and schedule your appointment.
